# BERT with a regression head, fine-tuned

Generated by `bert_based/run_finetune.py`. Model: `TurkuNLP/bert-base-finnish-cased-v1`.

## Summary

- BERT, fine-tuned with LoRA, trained on real-Finnish rows (2,332 texts): average miss **0.291 (5.8%)** [0.267, 0.319] on the test set (epoch 3 of 3, chosen on the real-Finnish valid texts).
- BERT, fully fine-tuned, trained on real-Finnish rows (2,332 texts): average miss **0.136 (2.7%)** [0.119, 0.157] on the test set (epoch 3 of 3, chosen on the real-Finnish valid texts).

## How to read the numbers

- **Average miss (MAE)**: average distance between predicted and true label; smaller is better. One label step is 0.5. A percent in brackets after an average miss is that miss divided by the label range (5 points, labels 1 to 6).
- **Exactly right / within one step**: the prediction, rounded to the nearest half step, equals the label / is off by at most 0.5.
- **95% interval**: from resampling the 865 test texts; overlapping intervals may be noise.

## Setup

- BERT with one linear output (regression), trained with mean squared error on the label standardised by the training mean and spread, AdamW, linear warm-up and decay.
- **LoRA**: the BERT weights are frozen; small low-rank adapters (rank 8) on the attention query and value matrices are trained, together with the head. 295,681 of 124,818,434 parameters (0.2%) are trained. Learning rate 0.0002, effective batch size 16, 3 epochs.
- **Full**: every weight is trained (124,522,753 parameters). Learning rate 2e-05, effective batch size 16, 3 epochs.
- Training examples are the windows of at most 510 word-pieces of every training text; a text is predicted as the average over its windows.
- The epoch is chosen on the real-Finnish valid texts (263), so the test set is never used to choose anything.
- Test set: the same 865 real-Finnish texts as in the curated results, so the numbers are comparable with `curated_features/modeling/reports/RESULTS.md`.
