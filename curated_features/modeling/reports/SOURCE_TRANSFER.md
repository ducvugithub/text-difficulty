## Summary

- Original test set: the normal model misses by **0.257 (5.1%)**. With one source left out of training: 0.268 (5.4%) to 0.473 (9.5%).
- On the left-out source itself, the curated features beat the surface baseline in 1 of 4 cases (`yle`).
- `yle` and `HS` are graded well, since they share a label (5.5). `easy_text` and `yle-selkouutiset` are graded badly, since no other source has their label.

## What this tests

Four of the five real-Finnish sources have one fixed label each, so a model can score well just by recognising the source. Here each of those four sources is left out of training in turn.

- **Feature set**: vocab + grammar_coverage + cognitive (new) (311 features), gradient boosting, trained as in `RESULTS.md` but without the left-out source.
- **Test set**: the original 865 test texts, as in `RESULTS.md`.
- **Excl. set**: all texts of the left-out source. This is the detail.
- `FI` stays in training and in the test set; it is never left out.
- **MAE**: average miss, smaller is better (one label step is 0.5). A percent in brackets after an average miss is that miss divided by the label range (5 points, labels 1 to 6). **Exactly acc**: share of predictions exactly right. **Within 0.5 acc**: share off by at most 0.5.

## How to read it

- **Test vs excl.**: the test-set columns are the normal evaluation, so they stay comparable with `RESULTS.md`. The excl. columns show how the model does on a source it has never seen; the gap between the two is the cost of a new source.
- **A source can only be graded as well as the labels the model has seen.** The other sources never have label 1.5 or 3.5, and 5.5 appears only in `yle` and `HS`. So excluding `easy_text` (1.5) or `yle-selkouutiset` (3.5) asks the model for a label it has never been trained on; a large miss there is expected.
- **`yle` and `HS` share the label 5.5**, so excluding one still leaves the other in training. Those two rows are the easiest and prove the least.
